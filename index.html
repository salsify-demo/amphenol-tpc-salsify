Why it matters now

Specs are the product

A buyer chooses TPC over commodity cable because of jacket material, temperature range, flex life, voltage rating and approvals. If a distributor lists one of those wrong, the comparison is lost. Salsify governs the technical record once and publishes it to every channel in the channel's format.

Distributor listings

Electrical and industrial distributors re-key TPC products into their own catalogs, with their own attribute sets.

Market-specific content

The same cable is sold into mining and into food processing with different proof points and compliance notes.

Case studies and proof

Savings stories and application photos are the sales content, and they need to travel with the product.

The pattern we see

Where product content usually breaks down

We have not audited Amphenol TPC's systems. What follows is the pattern we see at engineered component manufacturers selling through distribution and the questions we would like to check against yours.

Technical content in silos

  • Specs live with engineering, copy with marketing, case studies on a shared drive
  • Market-specific versions are built by hand for each sales deck
  • The website, distributor feeds and literature rarely match

Distributor re-keying

  • Each distributor wants a different attribute set for the same cable
  • Spec updates reach distributors late or not at all
  • Errors surface on the distributor's site, where TPC cannot see them
